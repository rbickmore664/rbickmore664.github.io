The right brace, in one sentence.

Describe your patient and their condition. Kinetiq finds the best-fit brace and recommends a size, then you order in seconds.

Tip: don't include names or birthdates. Age, sex, height, weight and condition are enough.

How it works

  1. 1

    Describe

    Type or speak one sentence: age, sex, height, weight and the condition.

  2. 2

    Match

    We score every brace in the catalog by body area, indications and fit, and recommend a size.

  3. 3

    Order

    Pick a size and side, add an internal reference, and check out. Only the minimum necessary info is collected.
