Frequently asked questions

Illustrative mock-up content. Policies below are examples, not real terms.

Who is Medicorp for?

Medicorp (a placeholder brand in this mock-up) is designed for physicians, physical therapists and clinic staff who need to choose and order orthopedic braces for patients. It is not a consumer diagnosis tool.

How does the brace finder work?

You describe the patient in one sentence (age, sex, height, weight and condition). The finder matches the description to braces in the catalog by body area and listed indications, then suggests a size from the manufacturer-style sizing chart. The prescribing clinician makes the final choice.

How is brace fitting and sizing handled?

Each product lists sizes with fit ranges (for example thigh circumference) plus height and weight guidance. The suggested size is a starting point; clinicians should confirm fit with a measurement and fitting at the point of care.

Can braces be paid for with insurance, HSA or FSA?

Illustrative answer: many prescribed orthopedic braces may be eligible for insurance reimbursement or HSA/FSA payment depending on the plan and documentation. HCPCS codes shown in this mock-up are examples only; check coverage with the payer.

What is the return policy?

Illustrative answer: unused braces in original packaging could be returned within 30 days; custom-fitted or used items may not be returnable. This mock-up does not process real returns.

How fast is shipping?

Illustrative answer: in-stock items would ship within 1 to 2 business days, with expedited options for clinics. No real orders are shipped from this mock-up.

Is this a real store?

No. This is a demonstration mock-up. The company name, products, prices, HCPCS codes and contact details are placeholders, and checkout uses a test card only. Nothing here is medical advice.
